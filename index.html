Content Agents

Content Agents is a personal automation system built and operated by a single individual developer. It researches topics, writes short-form video scripts, assembles the final videos, reviews them for safety and accuracy, and publishes the finished content to social media accounts that I personally own and manage.

What Content Agents does

The system runs a pipeline of specialized steps, each responsible for one part of the content lifecycle. No step publishes anything without passing through an automated safety review first.

1. Research

Identifies relevant, timely topics and content angles for short-form video.

2. Script

Writes a short, conversational script for each video, tailored to the topic.

3. Assembly

Renders a finished vertical video, combining footage and on-screen captions.

4. Safety Review

Every script and video is checked automatically before publishing — for factual accuracy, tone, and platform guideline compliance — before it is ever posted.

5. Publishing

The finished, approved video is published to my own connected TikTok account through the official Content Posting API.

Who operates this

Content Agents is a single-user personal project. I am the sole developer, owner, and operator of the system and of every connected account. No third party has access to this application, and it is not offered as a service to anyone else. The system runs on infrastructure I personally control.

Data and privacy

The application accesses only what is required to publish content to my own connected account, through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. Full details are available in the Privacy Policy.
