Personal automation project

Content Agents Publisher

A small pipeline of AI agents that researches, writes, and assembles short-form video content, then publishes it directly to my own TikTok account via the official Content Posting API.

01

Research & Script

Identifies content angles and writes a short-form script for each video.

02

Assembly

Renders a vertical video with stock footage and on-screen captions via FFmpeg.

03

Publish

Uploads the finished video to my own connected TikTok account through the Content Posting API.
