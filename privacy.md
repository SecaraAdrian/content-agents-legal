# Privacy Policy — Content Agents Publisher

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Overview

Content Agents Publisher ("the App") is a personal automation tool that publishes short-form video content to TikTok accounts owned and operated by the developer. The App is used solely to manage the developer's own TikTok accounts and is not offered as a public service to third parties.

## Data We Access

Through TikTok's Content Posting API, the App accesses only the following, and only for the developer's own connected accounts:

- Basic account identifiers required for authentication (OAuth 2.0)
- Permission to publish video content directly to the connected account
- Account settings relevant to publishing (privacy level, comment/duet/stitch permissions, maximum video duration) to ensure uploads comply with the account's own configuration

## Data We Do Not Access

The App does not access, collect, or store:

- Direct messages
- Personal contact information beyond what TikTok's OAuth flow provides
- Data belonging to other TikTok users
- Analytics data beyond what is needed to confirm successful publishing

## How Data Is Used

Access tokens and account data are used exclusively to publish video content prepared by the developer to the developer's own TikTok account(s). No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

## Data Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored securely on infrastructure controlled by the developer and are not shared with any external party. Tokens can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App from the TikTok Developer Portal or from TikTok account settings.

## Data Retention

Access tokens and related account data are retained only as long as the App is actively connected to the account. Data is deleted upon disconnection.
